# PMP® Exam Preparation and Business Application

## Cinematic Learner Experience Visual Brief

**Property of Obserra. Obserra proprietary information. Not for distribution.**

## Experience goal

The learner should feel as though they are entering a premium project command environment where decisions, evidence, people, value, risk, schedules, costs, governance, and outcomes are visible and interactive. The visual experience must not resemble a generic slide deck, a grid of static status cards, or an imitation of PMI’s examination interface.

## Official visual system

- Black and deep navy foundation.
- Gold for authority, evidence, ownership, and active decisions.
- Restrained holographic blue for data, systems, and AI-supported analysis.
- Green, amber, and red only for accessible, evidence-defined status.
- High-contrast modern sans-serif typography.
- Official owner-approved Obserra mark only.
- No generic shield, key, eye, or AI-human substitute marks.
- No unapproved bright consumer palette.
- No decorative type that reduces comprehension.

## Learner shell

The course player uses an immersive full-width workspace with five persistent capabilities:

1. **Cinematic lesson canvas** — video, interactive scenes, data graphics, and decision pauses.
2. **Learning command rail** — exact module sequence, progress, protected-assessment state, and source-version state.
3. **Evidence drawer** — authoritative source, publication date, retrieval date, source type, lesson use, and limitations.
4. **Workbook workspace** — project artifact, calculation, case analysis, or decision record for the current module.
5. **Obserrian PMP Coach** — official Obserra mark as the assistant identity, entitlement gated, course scoped, and locked during protected assessment.

The player must not display decorative cards that imply functionality without interaction. Every visible status, source, action, artifact, and recommendation must open a focused detail view or perform a real authorized action.

## Opening sequence

- Gold Obserra ident forms from light, data, and a project-network line.
- Course title appears over a cinematic enterprise command environment.
- Required proprietary, AI-native, paid-access, and internal-review or public-release tags appear.
- Short disclaimer and independent-provider statement appear long enough to read.
- Lesson objective and source baseline appear before substantive instruction.

## Recurring cinematic environments

### Project command center

A central table displays objective, value, stakeholders, delivery approach, scope, schedule, cost, risk, quality, compliance, change, and benefits. The table updates as evidence changes.

### Stakeholder field

People, influence, interest, impact, trust, and communication pathways appear as a living network. Selecting a stakeholder opens the evidence, expectation, engagement, and decision-right detail.

### Integrated planning room

Predictive schedules, adaptive backlogs, hybrid milestones, dependencies, resources, procurement, and governance appear as connected layers rather than disconnected documents.

### Risk and issue theater

Future uncertainty, realized issues, impediments, assumptions, constraints, dependencies, triggers, responses, residual risk, and verification are visually distinct.

### Benefits and value chamber

Outputs, adoption, outcomes, operating measures, customer value, and benefits ownership are separated so learners can see why completion does not automatically equal value.

### Examination simulation

Minimal, distraction-controlled, accessible interface with three protected sections, two break gates, visible time, flagging, confidence capture, and post-submission analytics. It must be visually original and must not recreate PMI’s examination screens.

## Data visualization standard

Every chart must show:

- Title and business question.
- Unit.
- Baseline or comparison.
- Observation date.
- Source identifier.
- Confidence or limitation where material.
- Accessible text equivalent.

Animated data must pause on interaction and provide a reduced-motion version.

## Public-case treatment

Documented cases use original timelines, diagrams, maps, and charts derived from verified public records. Each sequence displays:

- Official source owner.
- Source title and date.
- What the source establishes.
- What the source does not establish.
- Relevant ECO task.
- Transfer lesson.

Synthetic or AI-generated reenactments must be labeled `ILLUSTRATIVE REENACTMENT — NOT DOCUMENTARY FOOTAGE`.

## AI tutor presence

The tutor appears as the official Obserra mark with subtle pulse and evidence-linked speech, not as a generic humanoid assistant. The tutor panel displays:

- Current course and module.
- Source version.
- Progress evidence.
- Suggested learning action.
- Confidence and limitation.
- Feedback controls.
- Protected-assessment lock state.

During graded assessment, the tutor becomes visibly locked and exposes only technical support, timing, rules, exit, and submission controls.

## Interaction standard

Every module includes at least:

- One evidence inspection.
- One consequential decision.
- One answer-before-explanation pause.
- One business artifact.
- One original exam-style practice interaction.
- One AI tutor handoff after ungraded activity.
- One limitations statement.

## Accessibility

- WCAG-aligned contrast.
- Keyboard access to every interactive function.
- Screen-reader names, roles, states, and instructions.
- Captions and transcript.
- Audio description for essential visuals.
- Reduced-motion option.
- No color-only status.
- Adjustable playback speed and text size.
- Pause, rewind, chapter, and transcript search.
- Accessible alternatives for drag-and-drop and point-and-click interactions.

## Release rule

The visual concept may not be described as Hollywood-quality production until mastered media exists and passes factual, brand, accessibility, rights, technical, and owner review. Before that point, the accurate term is `cinematic production design and script package`.
