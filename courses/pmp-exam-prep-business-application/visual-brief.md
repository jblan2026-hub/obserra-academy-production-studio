# PMP® Exam Preparation and Business Application

## Cinematic Learner Experience Visual Brief

**Property of Obserra. Obserra proprietary information. Not for distribution.**

## Experience goal

The learner enters a premium project command environment where decisions, evidence, people, value, risk, schedules, costs, governance, and outcomes are visible and interactive. The experience must not resemble a generic slide deck, a grid of static cards, or an imitation of PMI’s examination interface.

## Official visual system

- Black and deep navy foundation.
- Gold for authority, evidence, ownership, and active decisions.
- Restrained holographic blue for data, systems, and AI-supported analysis.
- Accessible green, amber, and red only for evidence-defined status.
- High-contrast typography.
- Official owner-approved Obserra mark only.
- No generic shield, key, eye, human-avatar, or AI substitute mark.

## Learner shell

The course player provides five persistent, functional areas:

1. **Cinematic lesson canvas** — video, interactive scenes, data graphics, and decision pauses.
2. **Learning command rail** — exact lesson sequence, progress, protected-assessment state, and source version.
3. **Evidence drawer** — source owner, publication date, retrieval date, source type, lesson use, and limitations.
4. **Workbook workspace** — the project artifact, calculation, case analysis, or decision record for the current lesson.
5. **Obserrian PMP Coach** — official Obserra mark as the assistant identity, entitlement gated, course scoped, and locked during protected assessment.

Every visible status or action must open a focused detail view or perform a real authorized action. Decorative nonfunctional status cards are prohibited.

## Recurring cinematic environments

- Project command table connecting objective, value, stakeholders, approach, scope, schedule, cost, risk, quality, compliance, change, and benefits.
- Stakeholder field showing influence, impact, trust, expectations, engagement, and decision rights.
- Integrated planning room combining predictive schedules, adaptive backlogs, hybrid milestones, dependencies, resources, procurement, and governance.
- Risk and issue theater separating uncertainty, realized issues, impediments, assumptions, constraints, dependencies, triggers, responses, residual risk, and verification.
- Benefits chamber separating output, adoption, outcome, operating measure, and accountable benefit ownership.
- Protected examination simulation using an original, accessible interface with three sections, two break gates, timing, flagging, confidence capture, and post-submission analytics.

## Data visualization standard

Every chart must show:

- Business question.
- Unit.
- Baseline or comparison.
- Observation date.
- Source identifier.
- Confidence or limitation where material.
- Accessible text equivalent.

Animated data must pause on interaction and provide a reduced-motion version.

## Public-case treatment

Documented cases use original timelines, diagrams, maps, and charts derived from verified public records. Every sequence distinguishes:

- What the official source establishes.
- What the source does not establish.
- Contributing factors.
- Relevant ECO task.
- Transfer lesson.

Synthetic reenactments must be labeled `ILLUSTRATIVE REENACTMENT — NOT DOCUMENTARY FOOTAGE`.

## AI tutor presence

The tutor appears as the official Obserra mark with subtle pulse and evidence-linked speech. It displays:

- Current course and lesson.
- Source version.
- Progress evidence.
- Suggested learning action.
- Confidence and limitations.
- Feedback controls.
- Protected-assessment lock state.

During graded assessment, the tutor exposes only technical support, timing, rules, exit, and submission controls.

## Signature visual themes

- Eligibility: four-path hall and non-overlapping experience timeline.
- Exam architecture: 180-node field, three sections, two break gates, domain and approach overlays.
- Vision and stakeholders: living outcome and influence network.
- Conflict and leadership: interests, evidence, decision rights, and facilitated resolution.
- Integrated plan: connected planning layers.
- Scope and value: predictive boundary, adaptive backlog, output-to-outcome bridge.
- Resources and procurement: capacity and commercial-risk allocation.
- Finance and schedule: baselines, reserves, earned value, network logic, float, critical path, and simulation.
- Quality and compliance: prevention, inspection, failure cost, obligations, controls, and evidence.
- Governance and change: decision rights, thresholds, impact analysis, and authorized updates.
- Risk and improvement: cause-event-effect, response, residual risk, lessons, and measured change.
- AI and sustainability: governed AI decision record and lifecycle-effects map.
- Capstone: integrated global transformation command exercise.
- Readiness: domain, task, approach, format, timing, confidence, and remediation map.

## Interaction standard

Every lesson includes:

- Evidence inspection.
- Consequential decision.
- Answer-before-explanation pause.
- Business artifact.
- Original exam-style practice.
- AI tutor handoff after ungraded work.
- Limitations statement.

## Accessibility

- WCAG-aligned contrast.
- Keyboard operation.
- Screen-reader names, roles, states, and instructions.
- Captions and transcript.
- Audio description for essential visuals.
- Reduced-motion option.
- No color-only status.
- Adjustable playback speed and text size.
- Accessible alternatives for drag-and-drop and point-and-click.

## Release rule

The experience may not be described as Hollywood-quality production until mastered media exists and passes factual, brand, accessibility, rights, technical, and owner review. Before that, the accurate term is `cinematic production design and script package`.
