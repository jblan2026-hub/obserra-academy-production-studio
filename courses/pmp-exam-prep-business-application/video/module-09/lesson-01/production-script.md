# Lesson 33 Production and Learner-Experience Script

## Protected Practice Examination, Section One

**Course:** PMP® Exam Preparation and Business Application  
**Module:** 09, Protected Examination and Final Readiness  
**Planned learner time:** 80 minutes  
**Assessment length:** 60 original questions  
**Status:** Internal production draft  
**Classification:** OBSERRA PROPRIETARY INFORMATION. NOT FOR DISTRIBUTION.

## Purpose

Section One begins the original 180-question Obserra readiness simulation. It is designed to reproduce the cognitive and time-management demands of a long scenario-based examination without copying official PMI questions, interface designs, courseware, or protected test-bank content.

## Pre-section readiness screen

The learner must confirm:

1. The full 80-minute section can be completed without interruption.
2. The learner understands that the AI tutor and answer explanations are locked until submission.
3. The learner will not use external answer services, exam dumps, or unauthorized assistance.
4. Accessibility settings are correct.
5. The learner understands that moving to the scheduled break closes Section One review.

## Section architecture

1. 60 original items.
2. Mixed People, Process, and Business Environment coverage.
3. Predictive, adaptive or agile, and hybrid contexts.
4. Single-response, multiple-response, matching, sequencing, graphic interpretation, and case-set items using original Obserra interfaces.
5. One section timer.
6. Flag-for-review capability.
7. Keyboard navigation and screen-reader labels.
8. No answer rationale before submission.
9. No AI tutor assistance.
10. Immutable attempt record with question version and response timing.

## Timing model

The 240-minute practice simulation is divided into three 80-minute sections. Learners may use a personal pacing model such as:

1. First pass: approximately 60 to 65 minutes.
2. Flagged review: approximately 10 to 15 minutes.
3. Final unanswered-item check: approximately 3 to 5 minutes.

The learner is reminded that an average of 80 seconds per question is a planning reference, not a per-item limit.

## Section One content emphasis

The item-selection blueprint should emphasize:

1. Common vision and stakeholder alignment.
2. Leadership, empowerment, roles, and team performance.
3. Conflict, communication, and knowledge transfer.
4. Tailoring predictive, adaptive, and hybrid delivery.
5. Integrated planning and scope.
6. Introductory schedule, resource, risk, and value decisions.
7. Governance and authority recognition.

The exact item distribution must still preserve the complete 180-question domain and delivery-approach blueprint across all three sections.

## Learner reasoning reminder

Before beginning, the system displays the Obserra decision lens:

1. Environment.
2. Evidence.
3. Authority.
4. Sequence.
5. Value and protection.
6. Verification.

The reminder disappears when the assessment begins.

## During the section

The application must:

1. Save each response securely.
2. Preserve unanswered and flagged state.
3. Warn before accidental navigation away.
4. Prevent the course AI tutor from evaluating items or options.
5. Avoid exposing correct answers in page source, client state, logs, analytics, or error messages.
6. Record start, response, review, and submission timing under minimum-necessary privacy rules.
7. Provide accessible alternative interaction for graphics, drag-and-drop, and point-and-click items.
8. Maintain deterministic item and option ordering for later review of the exact attempt.

## End-of-section review

Before submission, the learner sees:

1. Answered count.
2. Unanswered count.
3. Flagged count.
4. Remaining time.
5. A clear warning that beginning the break closes the section.

The system does not identify likely correct or incorrect responses.

## Submission behavior

After submission:

1. Responses become immutable for the attempt.
2. The learner enters the scheduled break.
3. Section-level score may remain hidden until the full 180-question simulation is complete, according to the approved assessment policy.
4. Correct answers and detailed rationales remain protected until the approved feedback stage.
5. The attempt record stores item version, learner response, time, confidence when collected, and integrity events.

## Scheduled break one

A ten-minute break screen displays:

1. Section One is closed.
2. The learner cannot return to its items.
3. The Section Two timer has not begun.
4. The learner may resume early.
5. No course content, AI tutor, or answer explanation is displayed during the break.

## Accessibility requirements

1. Full keyboard operation.
2. Screen-reader question and option structure.
3. Non-color flag and status indicators.
4. Adjustable text size and contrast.
5. Accessible tables, formulas, networks, and graphics.
6. Reduced-motion mode.
7. Approved accommodations without weakening assessment integrity.
8. Time warnings announced accessibly.

## Security and integrity requirements

1. Server-side answer key and scoring.
2. Entitlement and named-learner scope.
3. Attempt token and expiration.
4. No answer data in browser bundles.
5. Rate limits and anomaly logging.
6. Course tutor lockout.
7. Audit record for start, resume, submit, timeout, and integrity events.
8. Originality and source record for each item.
9. Versioned assessment package.
10. No third-party analytics capturing item text or responses.

## Final production gate

Section One may enter owner final review only when all 60 assigned items are original, source mapped, technically validated, psychometrically reviewed as appropriate, accessible, securely delivered, and included in the exact staged learner package. No script-only preview may enter the owner queue.
