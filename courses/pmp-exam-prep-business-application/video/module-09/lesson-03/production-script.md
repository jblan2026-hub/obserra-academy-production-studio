# Lesson 35 Production and Learner-Experience Script

## Protected Practice Examination, Section Three and Final Readiness Review

**Course:** PMP® Exam Preparation and Business Application  
**Module:** 09, Protected Examination and Final Readiness  
**Planned learner time:** 80 minutes  
**Assessment length:** 60 original questions plus post-submission readiness review  
**Status:** Internal production draft  
**Classification:** OBSERRA PROPRIETARY INFORMATION. NOT FOR DISTRIBUTION.

## Purpose

Section Three completes the original 180-question Obserra readiness simulation and transitions the learner from protected testing to evidence-based remediation, study planning, and business application.

## Re-entry screen

The learner confirms:

1. The first 120 questions are closed.
2. Section Three contains the final 60 original questions.
3. The AI tutor and answer explanations remain locked until final submission.
4. The learner has 80 minutes for the final section.
5. Final submission ends the protected attempt and begins the readiness analysis.

## Section Three content emphasis

1. Project governance and decision rights.
2. Compliance and controlled change.
3. Issues, impediments, and recovery.
4. Risk and opportunity.
5. Continuous improvement and organizational change.
6. External business environment.
7. AI-supported project work and human oversight.
8. Sustainability and stakeholder value.
9. Closure, transition, and benefits.
10. Fully integrated cross-domain scenarios.
11. Predictive, adaptive or agile, and hybrid settings.
12. Original enhanced item formats and case sets.

## Integrated case-set model

A final case may present an enterprise transformation with:

1. Stakeholder conflict.
2. Schedule and cost variance.
3. Supplier delay.
4. A new regulatory requirement.
5. AI output risk.
6. Low adoption.
7. Accessibility and quality findings.
8. A sponsor request to preserve launch timing.

Each question changes one fact or asks for a different decision. The learner must distinguish stable facts from newly introduced evidence and avoid carrying an invalid answer assumption into later questions.

## Protected delivery behavior

The application maintains:

1. Server-side answer key and scoring.
2. Named-learner entitlement.
3. Secure and resumable state within approved policy.
4. Deterministic item version and response record.
5. Tutor and rationale lockout.
6. Accessible graphics and interaction alternatives.
7. No third-party item telemetry.
8. Clear timeout and final-submission confirmation.
9. Audit records for integrity events.
10. Privacy-minimized performance analytics.

## Final submission warning

The learner sees:

1. Answered count.
2. Unanswered count.
3. Flagged count.
4. Time remaining.
5. A statement that final submission ends the complete attempt.
6. Confirmation that remediation and rationale become available only after submission according to policy.

## Scoring and analytics

The protected scoring service calculates:

1. Overall percentage for the original simulation.
2. Performance by People, Process, and Business Environment.
3. Performance by ECO task.
4. Predictive, adaptive or agile, and hybrid performance.
5. Question-format performance.
6. Calculation and graphic-interpretation performance.
7. First-pass versus changed-answer performance when governed.
8. Time distribution by section and item type.
9. Confidence calibration when collected.
10. Recurring reasoning-pattern errors.

The result must not be represented as an official PMI score, passing prediction, or guarantee.

## Rationale release

After full submission, the learner may review original Obserra items according to the protected-assessment policy. Review includes:

1. Learner response.
2. Correct response.
3. Why it is strongest.
4. Why each distractor is weaker.
5. Domain, task, and lesson mapping.
6. Approved source identifiers.
7. Reasoning pattern.
8. Business application.
9. Limitations.
10. Recommended remediation.

The system should limit bulk extraction and preserve paid-course and proprietary protections.

## Readiness categories

The course may use internal readiness categories such as:

### Strong readiness evidence

Consistent domain performance, sound timing, stable reasoning, and successful full simulation under protected conditions.

### Conditional readiness

Adequate overall performance with identifiable task, environment, timing, or reasoning gaps requiring focused remediation.

### Additional preparation recommended

Material gaps across domains, timing, scenario interpretation, calculations, or assessment integrity.

These labels are Obserra learning guidance, not PMI eligibility or pass predictions.

## Personalized remediation plan

The system produces:

1. Weakest tasks and domains.
2. Most frequent reasoning errors.
3. Required lesson review.
4. Targeted original practice.
5. Formula and artifact exercises.
6. Spaced-review schedule.
7. Full-section retest timing.
8. Exam-day pacing and break plan.
9. Business-application assignments.
10. Readiness re-evaluation date.

## Obserrian PMP Coach unlock

After submission, the tutor may:

1. Explain missed concepts.
2. Discuss why options were stronger or weaker.
3. Generate new ungraded practice questions.
4. Build a study plan.
5. Coach business application.
6. Compare delivery approaches.
7. Review learner-created artifacts.
8. Cite approved sources.

It may not claim that the learner will pass, disclose protected items outside permitted review, or provide recalled official examination content.

## Final learner deliverable

The learner completes a Readiness and Business Application Plan containing:

1. Intended examination date.
2. Domain and task priorities.
3. Study cadence.
4. Practice and simulation schedule.
5. Timing strategy.
6. Integrity commitment.
7. Business artifact to apply at work.
8. Knowledge-transfer plan.
9. Continuing professional-development objective.
10. Limitations and conditions that would change the readiness decision.

## Completion gate

Course completion requires:

1. All instructional lessons completed.
2. Required practice and business artifacts completed.
3. The complete 180-question protected simulation submitted.
4. The course-defined passing standard satisfied where applicable.
5. Required integrity acknowledgements completed.
6. No unresolved entitlement or assessment-integrity block.
7. Completion record and certificate metadata generated through the governed learner platform.

The Obserra certificate confirms course completion only. It is not the PMP credential, official PMI training approval, examination eligibility, licensure, academic credit, compliance validation, or a guarantee of professional outcome.

## Final review gate

The complete course enters the Academy admin final-review queue only when:

1. All 35 final learner units exist in the staged package.
2. All mastered videos have audible verified audio, captions, transcripts, and accessibility alternatives.
3. All 180 original items and answer rationales pass review.
4. The AI tutor is entitlement-scoped and assessment-aware.
5. Paid learner access, progress, breaks, scoring, remediation, and certificate workflows are operational.
6. Source, rights, legal, brand, accessibility, security, media, psychometric, commerce, and owner-precheck gates pass.
7. The admin page renders the exact staged learner experience.
8. Owner approval does not publish automatically.

No script, static mockup, silent prototype, or partial course may enter final review.
