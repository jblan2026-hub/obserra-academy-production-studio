# Cybersecurity Foundations Workbook

## Exercise 1: Identify the business consequence

Choose a common workplace asset such as a customer record, executive mailbox, manufacturing system, financial approval process, or employee identity. Document:

- The business purpose of the asset
- The people or operations that depend on it
- The consequences of unauthorized disclosure
- The consequences of unauthorized change
- The consequences of unavailability
- The approved owner or escalation path

## Exercise 2: Identity decision

You receive three unexpected multifactor prompts. Record the safest immediate actions, the evidence you would preserve, and the team you would notify.

## Exercise 3: Suspicious request

A senior leader appears to request an urgent payment through email and asks you to avoid the normal approval workflow. Identify the warning signs and write the verification steps you would use without replying to the suspicious message.

## Exercise 4: Incident report

Draft a factual incident report using this structure:

- Date and time observed
- Reporter
- Systems, accounts, or information involved
- Known facts
- Actions already taken
- Evidence preserved
- Immediate business concern
- Requested assistance

## Personal action plan

List three changes you will make in your daily work to improve identity protection, threat recognition, and incident reporting.
