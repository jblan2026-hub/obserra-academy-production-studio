# Obserra Academy Brand and Handling Standard

All course-production assets are owned and issued by **OBSERRA EXECUTIVE PROTECTION & INTELLIGENCE LLC**.

## Mandatory branding

Every manuscript, learner guide, workbook, assessment, slide deck, visual, video, transcript, caption file, certificate, catalog record, and release package must use:

- The official Obserra logo and eye-key shield mark.
- The official Obserra black, dark navy, gold, and restrained holographic blue palette.
- The full legal company name where ownership or issuance is stated.
- Consistent executive-grade visual presentation.

## Proprietary classification

Draft, internal-review, provider-submitted, and pre-release assets must display:

**OBSERRA PROPRIETARY INFORMATION. NOT FOR DISTRIBUTION.**

This classification remains in place until an authorized release approval explicitly permits a public or learner-facing distribution version.

## External AI providers

External AI course and video providers receive only the minimum content required for the approved production job. Jobs must include the Obserra brand requirements and proprietary classification. API keys, generated media, and provider job artifacts must not be committed to source control.

## Certificates

Certificates must use the official Obserra brand, identify OBSERRA EXECUTIVE PROTECTION & INTELLIGENCE LLC as issuer, include a unique certificate identifier, identify the completed course, record the completion date, and support verification against the retained learner transcript.
