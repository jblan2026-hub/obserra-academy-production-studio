# Autonomous Patch Authority Boundary

- **Version:** 1.0.0
- **Status:** Mandatory Academy boundary
- **Effective date:** 2026-08-07

Course enrollment, learning activity, assessment submission, certificate issuance, catalog synchronization, or connection to an Academy application never grants patch, containment, remediation, or administrative authority over a learner, student, customer, instructor, reviewer, or vendor device or system.

Automated technical maintenance is limited to Obserra-owned Studio, build, packaging, release, catalog, website, and deployment assets, plus separately authorized customer-managed enterprise assets with an explicit administrative authority record.

Learner and student devices, customer personal devices, browsers, instructor personal devices, vendor and supplier systems, payment, identity, email, analytics, hosting, content-delivery, collaboration, and external learning-platform infrastructure, and unknown assets remain observe-only.

The Studio may rebuild and patch its own governed runtime, protect its own sessions and APIs, or notify responsible owners. It may not mutate connected learner or vendor systems. Human approval for dependency outage risk cannot create patch authority over a prohibited target.
