# PMP® Exam Preparation and Business Application

## Learner Workbook — Production Draft 0.1.0

**Property of Obserra. Obserra proprietary information. Not for distribution.**

This workbook supports an independent Obserra Academy course. It is not official PMI courseware, does not contain live examination questions, and does not determine official eligibility. Use the current PMI requirements and your organization’s approved policies and authority structure.

---

# Workbook instructions

For each activity:

1. Record the facts provided.
2. Identify assumptions and missing evidence.
3. Identify the delivery approach.
4. Identify decision authority.
5. Select the strongest first or next action.
6. Explain why at least two alternatives are weaker.
7. Identify the artifact or record that should be created or updated.
8. State how the outcome will be verified.
9. Record confidence from 1 to 5.
10. Revisit low-accuracy or high-confidence errors through the AI tutor after the activity is submitted.

Do not enter confidential, regulated, export-controlled, personal, or restricted organizational data unless your organization has approved the Academy workflow for that data.
