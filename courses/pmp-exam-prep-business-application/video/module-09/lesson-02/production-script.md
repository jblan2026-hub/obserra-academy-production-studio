# Lesson 34 Production and Learner-Experience Script

## Protected Practice Examination, Section Two

**Course:** PMP® Exam Preparation and Business Application  
**Module:** 09, Protected Examination and Final Readiness  
**Planned learner time:** 80 minutes  
**Assessment length:** 60 original questions  
**Status:** Internal production draft  
**Classification:** OBSERRA PROPRIETARY INFORMATION. NOT FOR DISTRIBUTION.

## Purpose

Section Two continues the original Obserra 180-question readiness simulation after the first scheduled break. It increases integrated Process and Business Environment complexity while continuing to test People-domain judgment and delivery-method tailoring.

## Re-entry screen

Before the timer begins, the learner sees:

1. Section One is closed and cannot be reopened.
2. Section Two contains 60 new original questions.
3. The AI tutor and answer explanations remain locked.
4. The learner should confirm accessibility and readiness settings.
5. Beginning Section Two starts the next 80-minute timer.

## Section Two content emphasis

The governed item blueprint should emphasize:

1. Integrated project-management planning.
2. Scope, backlog, requirements, and acceptance.
3. Value-based delivery and benefits.
4. Estimation, dependencies, critical path, and resources.
5. Procurement, supplier, finance, quality, and compliance.
6. Status metrics, forecasts, artifacts, and executive decisions.
7. Change, issues, impediments, and corrective action.
8. Stakeholder and team implications inside each process scenario.
9. Predictive, adaptive or agile, and hybrid environments.
10. Original calculations and graphic interpretation.

## Enhanced item treatments

Section Two may include original:

1. Schedule-network analysis.
2. Earned-value calculations.
3. Risk and reserve calculations.
4. Supplier evaluation matrices.
5. Requirement-traceability cases.
6. Status dashboards with hidden concentration risk.
7. Matching of authority to decision.
8. Sequencing of recovery or change-control actions.
9. Multiple-response controls or evidence selections.
10. Case sets that introduce new facts across several questions.

All interfaces must be original Obserra designs and must not reproduce the official PMP examination interface.

## Reasoning discipline

The learner is expected to:

1. Identify what changed.
2. Separate fact from assumption.
3. Determine whether the event is risk, issue, defect, change, or decision.
4. Identify the accountable role.
5. Select the strongest first or next action.
6. Preserve value, people, obligations, quality, and evidence.
7. Avoid unauthorized escalation or commitment.
8. Verify the result.

## During the section

The application must preserve the same secure and accessible behavior as Section One:

1. Server-side answer data only.
2. Secure response persistence.
3. Deterministic item and option versioning.
4. Flagged-item review.
5. Accessible formulas, tables, diagrams, and interaction alternatives.
6. No tutor answer assistance.
7. No rationale or score leakage.
8. Audit records for state changes and integrity events.
9. Protection against third-party capture of question content.
10. Clear timeout and submission behavior.

## Pacing support

The interface may display:

1. Questions completed.
2. Time remaining.
3. Average time used.
4. Flagged and unanswered counts.
5. Section-progress checkpoints.

It must not predict whether an answer is correct or recommend which option to choose.

## End-of-section review

The learner receives a neutral completeness screen. The application warns that beginning Break Two closes Section Two permanently for the attempt.

## Scheduled break two

The ten-minute break screen confirms:

1. Sections One and Two are closed.
2. Section Three has not begun.
3. No item review, tutor, lesson, or rationale is available.
4. The learner may resume early.
5. The final section includes the readiness-review submission that follows the last 60 questions.

## Assessment-quality requirements

Every Section Two item must include internal records for:

1. Domain and task.
2. Delivery environment.
3. Lesson mapping.
4. Source references.
5. Correct response.
6. Rationale.
7. Distractor rationale.
8. Cognitive level.
9. Difficulty target.
10. Originality attestation.
11. SME, technical, editorial, psychometric, and accessibility status.
12. Item performance after governed use.

## Final production gate

Section Two remains unavailable to owner final review until all assigned items, scoring behavior, accessibility, security, attempt persistence, break transition, and protected-delivery controls pass. The admin page must show the exact final learner interface, not this script or a prototype.
